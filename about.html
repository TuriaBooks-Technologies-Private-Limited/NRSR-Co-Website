Our Firm

Chartered Accountants Since July 2019

M/s NRSR & Co is a partnership firm of Chartered Accountants established in July 2019 in Karkala, now headquartered in Manipal, Udupi District, with a branch office in Bengaluru. The firm provides Chartered Accountant services within Karnataka and across state boundaries.

The firm's practice is guided by the following principles:

  • Integrity: Clear, transparent reporting is provided to clients from the outset of an engagement.
  • Transparency: Clients are kept updated periodically, with assessments shared before external filings.
  • Client Centricity: Each engagement is approached with attention to the client's specific financial and compliance requirements.
