Who We Are

About the Firm

M/s NRSR & Co is a partnership firm of Chartered Accountants providing financial, compliance, and technology-related services to businesses. The firm's practice covers direct and indirect taxation, corporate structuring, statutory audit, and financial advisory, supported by digital tools for document handling and workflow tracking.

How We Operate

Professional Approach to Service Delivery

We provide professional services through online modes, supported by structured processes for assignment management, client communication and data security. Our workflow is designed to facilitate timely and consistent handling of assignments and client requirements.

01

Structured Practice Management

We use Practice Management Software to manage and monitor assignments throughout their lifecycle, from creation of the client profile and initiation of the assignment to completion and delivery of the final deliverables.

02

Data Security and Backup

We follow data backup and security practices to support the integrity and availability of client information. The firm maintains regular online and offline backups as part of its data management process.

03

Accessible Client Communication

Client queries and communication can be managed through multiple channels, including the website, telephone, WhatsApp and email. The firm uses online processes to communicate relevant compliance requirements and updates to clients.
